# Zipora

## Users and purpose
Students, job seekers, office workers and creators worldwide who need a quick file task without an account. Nineteen live tool pages cover nine PDF tasks, nine image tasks and a QR generator, including target-size presets. Processing stays on the device and every download is a real file. General Remove Background and Watermark Remover remain Coming Soon.

## Brand personality
Brisk, precise, approachable. Brand spelling: Zipora. Tagline: Your files. Done in a flash.

## Current visual direction — 7 November 2026
The user's latest brief supersedes the earlier gradient removal. The homepage hero uses solid #2563EB with a black headline, dark search text and white supporting text for contrast; the warm Sunset Bloom treatment has been removed at the user’s request. All tool pages and the rest of the main site use white surfaces, dark text and #2563EB actions. Upload areas are dashed white panels; results use quiet green feedback. The footer is dark. The user-supplied blue ribbon-Z icon and bold Zipora wordmark PNG are shared by the header and footer. The wordmark is black on white and displayed white on the dark footer through CSS. Browser/home-screen icons and organization metadata retain the blue icon. Brand links keep the existing return-to-home/top behavior.

## Design and content principles
Keep upload and download obvious. Preserve all existing processing handlers. Explain real limits and failures. Support mobile, keyboard use and reduced motion. Search the grouped nineteen-tool catalog from the hero; the compact navbar has no search field. use four cards per desktop row and two per mobile row. Keep labelled Advertisement placeholders away from task controls; no active ad scripts.

## Trust and discovery
Use distinct tool metadata, one H1 per page, tool-specific FAQs/instructions, honest related links and sourced comparisons. No fake numbers, testimonials, users, features or signup walls. Contact remains a clearly labelled placeholder until a real public address is supplied. Brand and internal navigation do not depend on which domain is purchased; set the public URL at build time for sitemap/canonicals/schema.

## Accessibility
Semantic controls, visible focus, announced progress/errors, keyboard reordering, a working mobile menu, reduced motion and no horizontal overflow at 390px.

## Tool layout revision
No tool breadcrumbs or What next section. Related Tools appears above How to use. A small top ad precedes each H1; a sidebar ad sits below the desktop summary; a result ad appears below the real download area. The existing bottom ad after FAQ remains. Summary panels are hidden only on mobile; the passport framing preview remains in the mobile workspace. Compress PDF starts in Balanced mode.
